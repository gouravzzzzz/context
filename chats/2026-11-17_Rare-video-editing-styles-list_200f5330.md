## Combination styles

1. **"Drawn world" (3 + 2 + 8).** Talking head in a tracked 3D set, with a hand-drawn Grease Pencil character or arrows on top. It's graded to 16mm with film burns on the cuts. It reads like a mix of documentary and animation.
2. **"Broken documentary" (6 + 5 + 4).** Paper-cutout photos with 2.5D parallax, using datamosh as the transition between photos. Key words are written in cloth-sim handwriting instead of fading in. This fits your documentry style skill but is much harder to copy.
3. **"Splat walk-in" (7 + 1 + 2).** Fly through a splat-scanned room, then transition into a projected 3D set. A Blender object is composited in and carries the main point.
4. **"Tape and glitch" (5 + 8 + 4).** VHS footage that datamoshes on beat drops, with 3D physics text. It's rawer and aimed at music or hype content.
