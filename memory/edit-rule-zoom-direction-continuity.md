---
name: edit-rule-zoom-direction-continuity
description: GLOBAL: camera direction carries across every cut/transition — end on a zoom-in, the next shot starts zooming in (same for out, and for slides)
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

The camera direction carries across every cut and transition:
- If a shot ends on a zoom-in, or a zoom-through pushes in, the next shot **starts zooming IN** too, never with a zoom-out.
- If the next shot opens with a zoom-out, as speaker entries do, the outgoing shot's last move is a gentle zoom-out that lands before the cut.
- Slides keep their direction across the cut in the same way.

**Why:** Krish, 2026-11-19 (Ron short 02, Women → Solopreneur): "if we are zooming in at the end of the first animation so why the fuck do we have a zoom out in the next animation?? how many times do i have to tell you". This was already in the documentry skill ("transitions are pure… the next photo keeps zooming IN") but was never global.

**How to apply:**
- This applies to every reel and every skill.
- When designing the camera, list every cut with the direction on each side: the last move of shot A and the first move of shot B.
- Print that list with a velocity check before rendering, and fix any IN→OUT or OUT→IN at a cut.
- Combine with [[edit-rule-transition-ease-pair]]: outgoing on graph 2, incoming continues on graph 1, both in the same direction.
