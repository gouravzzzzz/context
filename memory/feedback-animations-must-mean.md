Copy a reference's *look* (type, ground, glow, transitions), but design each animation so it shows what the line says. Use real assets that fit the line. Never use stock footage.

**Why:** Ron short 02 v2 (2026-11-19). A copied panel with a progress bar and gradient squares under "AI allows us the same advantages" "doesn't make sense at all". Krish said: "i want the aniamtions to be inspired by the looks of the reference animations but that doesnt mean you cant make your own animations". The B-roll was stock ("never fucking use stock"), and a shop photo was reused in two scenes.

**How to apply:**
- For each graphic, write down what the line means first. Then pick assets that show it:
  - real logos for named companies
  - the speaker's own company footage, from their YouTube interviews
  - real photos of the people named
- Never repeat a picture across scenes.
- Don't add a hook title unless asked.
- Speaker captions just fade in and stay until they're replaced. Run an automatic face-overlap check on them.
- Put shadows on all text.
