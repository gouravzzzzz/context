name: edit-rule-key-words-big-bold
description: Names, locations, numbers and expressions in captions must always be big, bold and highlighted, and every caption needs a clear shadow + glow
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

In every reel's captions, put **names, locations, numbers and expressions** in big, bold, highlighted type. Give them their own hero line (bold gold, larger) or the highlight box. Never leave them in the small regular lead line. Every caption also gets a clear soft drop shadow **and** a glow, so it reads on any background.

**Why:** User notes on Ron_TH_001_RL v2 (2026-11-15): "the 'indianapolis' word should be big and bold as its a location, always remember to make these text big and bold and highlighted such as locations, names, expressions, numbers, etc." and "all the texts should have a good shadow to make it visible and a glow as well just like in the references".

**How to apply:** When planning caption pages, scan every line for names, places, numbers and expressions, and move them to a hero or box line. This holds in every style ([[skill-documentry-style]], flourish). It is still subject to [[edit-rule-fonts-no-big-bold]] where a project sets its own type rules, and to [[edit-rule-reels-safe-zone]].
