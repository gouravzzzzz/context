- Every zoom in/out and pan lasts about 2 to 3 seconds on the user's ease graph ([[ease-graph-user-smooth]]). Moves of about 1 s on that graph look too fast.
- Text and captions appear a little before the word is spoken and stay on long enough to read. Never let them vanish quickly.
- Every time photos are on screen they get a vignette.
- Photo sequences can pan slowly (sideways and up from the bottom) instead of zooming, when the user asks for it.

**Why:** User notes on CHASE_009 v4 preview 2 (2026-11-14): "your zooms are too fast they need to last atleast 2 to 3 secs", "the texts arent readable its disappearing so fast so fix that everywhere", "add vignette to everytime photos appear".

**How to apply:** When short photo beats can't hold a 2-3 s move each, run one continuous camera move across the whole sequence and cut or dissolve the photos inside it. Related: [[edit-rule-camera-movement]], [[zoom-reference-chase-th-008]].
