Every reel uses only **two colour families** that suit each other. Shades of those two are fine: dark, mid and light. That applies to all graphics, animations, captions and text. Never use a 3–5 colour mix.

**CHASE_009 (current reel): blue + golden.**
- **Blue family:** deep navy for backgrounds, mid blue for shapes, light ice-blue for secondary text.
- **Gold family:** rich gold for accents and key words, pale champagne/cream for primary text instead of pure white.
- v1 mixed cyan, orange-red, paper white and greys. That's out.

**Why:** User said on 2026-11-14: "only use golden and blue shades in everything… Don't use a variety of colors… Always use two colors… save this, remember this."
**How to apply:** At the start of any reel, pick or confirm the pair and define it as named tokens (2 families × a few shades). Then check every element against them, including glows, strokes, highlights and transitions. When the user hasn't named a pair, choose two that clearly complement each other and state the choice. Works with [[edit-rule-fonts-no-big-bold]] (one accent word gets the accent colour) and [[typography-board-reference]].
