Don't stand in for people or objects with flat placeholder graphics: plain circles for people, a white rectangle with grey lines for a document, and so on. Source real, good-looking assets from **Pinterest** (the user's account, via BrowserOS neo) and use those:
- **People:** flat, solid **silhouette person icons**: a round head and rounded shoulders, sometimes a businessman with a tie, filled in one palette colour. Use these, NOT photo cut-outs. The user rejected the duotone photo people in CHASE_009 v2 on 2026-11-14 and pointed to pins 685250899581888637, 1077275173336113062, 567383253070205213 and 677580706478353643. For CHASE_009 that's two figures for "you" vs "them / the other side", replacing v1's blue and orange discs.
- **Documents and paper:** real paper (scanned or photographed sheets, contracts, letters), replacing v1's fake drawn document on the table.

**Why:** User asked on 2026-11-14, reviewing v1's "OTHER SIDE" table shot: "Use real paper and the real person PNGs… I think that would look much better."
**How to apply:**
- Search Pinterest first and pick clean, high-resolution cut-outs.
- Tint or grade them so they stay inside the reel's two-colour palette ([[edit-rule-two-color-palette]]); for CHASE_009, you = blue and them = gold.
- Keep both people in one matching style.
- Keep the shapes' storytelling role: the PARTNER overlap becomes the two figures meeting or side by side.
- Everything stays in [[edit-rule-reels-safe-zone]].
