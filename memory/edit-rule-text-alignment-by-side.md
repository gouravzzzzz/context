---
name: edit-rule-text-alignment-by-side
description: GLOBAL, every reel and every style/skill: text placed on the left is left-aligned, on the right right-aligned, in the middle centred
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Align text by where it sits on the screen, in every reel and every style or skill:
- **Left side of the frame:** left-aligned. All lines share one left edge.
- **Right side:** right-aligned. All lines share one right edge.
- **Middle:** centred.

This covers captions, lead and main lines, labels and the boxes behind them. The box follows the text's edge.

**Why:** Krish had to point this out in the documentry skill and again in the Jennifer Musselman style (Ron short 02 v3, 2026-11-19): "i dont get why do i need to repeat this in every skill". A caption block centred in a left-side column looked wrong.

**How to apply:** this is a global rule, not a per-skill one. When building any caption or text system, derive the alignment from the block's position: anchor at x < ~380 → left, x > ~700 → right, else centre. Never centre-align a column that sits against one side. Centred means the **exact frame centre (x 540 on 1080)**. Never nudge a centred block sideways to fit the safe zone; make the column narrower instead (Krish circled Ron's chest captions sitting at x 470: "they should be at the very middle"). Check it in stills before showing, with a thin centre guide line on the check sheet. **The same goes for images:** a centred PNG or cut-out is centred on the PERSON (head and body), not on its bounding box. A laptop, arm or prop stretching the box must not push the person off-centre. Krish, 2026-11-19, solopreneur PNG: "why the fuck is that image not properly aligned to the middle".
