Photo moves:
- A sliding photo ends with its subject **centred**, not parked left or right.
- It keeps moving for the whole shot. Never let it hit the picture edge and stop.
- A push or wipe into the next photo goes **the same direction** the photos are sliding. Never the opposite way.
- Each photo must show what is being said at that moment. No stock-looking images and no tight face close-ups.

**Why:** User notes on Ron_TH_001_RL v2 (2026-11-15): "never do a transition opposite of the direction the photo is moving", "the photo needs to land in centre not right or left", "the photo at 37th sec stops moving mid way", "the photos need to [show] what is being said", "looks like stock and the face is too close to camera".

**How to apply:** Give both photos of a push pair one direction and derive the push side from it. Check that the slide path fits inside the image (zoom slightly or shorten the slide). Speaker shots get one smooth zoom-out with no restart at jump cuts. See [[skill-documentry-style]], [[edit-rule-camera-movement]].
