**Kinetic one-word style (Xeloria, VV):**
- Bold geometric sans, all lowercase (Proxima Nova / Gilroy type), tight negative tracking.
- One word per beat (about 0.25 s), centred. The background flash-cuts between black and white, or black and yellow, on every word.
- Stacked lockups with negative leading: a small word sits on top of a big word and touches it ("we" over "don't", "just" over "edit"). In 3-line stacks the lines are offset: "don't" small and left, "need" big, "content." small and pushed right.
- Phrases end with a period ("content.", "feeling.", "repeat.").
- Words come in with a 2–4 frame blur-to-sharp fade. Some are typed letter by letter with a thin caret ("don't", "repeat."). Yellow text on black gets a soft glow.

**Editorial story style (EarnEdits):**
- *Small subtitle lines:* regular grotesk, sentence case, small, top-left. They build word by word with a blur fade, and the second line is in an accent colour ("The most powerful AI / Anthropic ever built").
- *Size hierarchy:* a small sentence ("So they built a safer version") sits above one large key term ("Fable 5") that staggers in letter by letter.
- *Condensed bold caps stack:* flush right, tight leading (DID / THEY / GET / PUNISHED). Each word blurs in from a dim grey to white. A counter-line sits flush left at the bottom ("FOR BEING HONEST"), and only the key word is in the accent colour (mustard).
- *Heavy extended caps:* "TOO POWERFUL", "EDITORS". The key words are in yellow ("STAY ONLINE"), with a small regular lowercase line underneath. Exits use a horizontal motion-blur whip.

**Shared rules:**
- 1–4 words on screen at once.
- Big contrast in size and weight within one lockup.
- One accent word per lockup.
- Tight leading and tracking.
- Words arrive with a blur-in on the spoken word.
