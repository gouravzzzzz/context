Context: "The Flourish Feed" is a Dime Motion client; covers reels and episode cuts for Gillian Stovel Rivers' guests (e.g. Pam).

**01 Script:** Every card has a script doc. Black lines stay in, in the written order. Red struck lines come out. Never reorder, never add lines from the raw, never trim words still black. If a line won't cut cleanly (cough, crosstalk, clipped word) → leave a comment on the card with the timecode; don't change the script.

**02 Hook (0:00–0:04):** First 3–4 s are FULL-SCREEN B-roll built from the hook text on the card, with the hook text on top, then cut to the guest. Opening B-roll ALWAYS shows a famous person (celebrity, well-known business figure, public figure recognisable on sight) — no exceptions. Even if later lines lack B-roll, the hook always gets its own shot with a famous face.
Example (Pam reel 3, hook "YOU LOSE FRIENDS WHEN YOU RETIRE."): well-known person at their own retirement/farewell, then the empty desk next morning; full frame, graded, hook on top; cut to Pam at 0:04.

**03 B-roll — real, specific, colourful, never stock.** Should look like a documentary, not a template.
- USE: interviews, real-life footage, celebrity paparazzi footage, events/real-life events, documentaries, films that are not overused.
- SKIP: stock footage (esp. slow motion), generic office/laptop/handshake shots, very big films everyone has seen, flat/ungraded/washed-out clips.
- Guest footage first: use footage given on the card; else guest's own socials (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, website) — speaking, events, stage; then go wider.
- Start B-roll search on **Pinterest**.
- One grade for the whole clip: in Premiere, basic Lumetri on every B-roll piece, tweak until all match; vibrant colour. One warm shot next to one cold washed-out shot = fail.

**04 Meaning over words:** Work out what the line is really about and show that. Literal match = filler. If unsure ask: "What is the feeling or idea behind this line, and what real moment would show it?"
Example: line "You wake up and there's no emails other than junk emails, nobody's phoning." → meaning: your importance disappeared overnight → literal (SKIP): phone on table / empty inbox → meaning shot (USE): famous CEO walking out of the building on the last day with nobody waiting outside.

**05 Naming:** `ClientName_TH_00X` (TH = Talking Head; guest/client name from the card + 3-digit clip number), e.g. `Pam_TH_003`.

**06 Checklist before upload:**
- [ ] Every black line in, in order; every red line out.
- [ ] First 3–4 s full-screen B-roll showing the hook, hook text on top.
- [ ] Opening B-roll shows a famous person.
- [ ] Guest footage (given or from socials) used before anything else.
- [ ] No stock / slow-mo stock anywhere, no very big films.
- [ ] All B-roll graded vibrant, every shot matches the same grade.
- [ ] Each shot shows the meaning of its line, not the literal words.
- [ ] File named ClientName_TH_00X, uploaded to Frame.io, link pasted as a comment on the card.
Footer: every clip still goes through Gillian's compliance review before it posts.
