## Rules taken from it
- Depth order back → front: ground → headline → photo card → caption → speaker cut-out.
- Text is laid out once and reveals in place (fade 2–3 f for connector words, pop-fade for the bold word, letter-by-letter type-on for the hero words). Text stays fronto-parallel and straight; the 3D is all camera.
- One continuous camera move does the transition: slow in, fastest ~60 % through, short settle; foreground defocus grows with the move (focus racks from the speaker to the headline).
